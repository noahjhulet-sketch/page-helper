Blanks practice worksheet

For testing the buddy's Blanks tool. Open this in your browser, snip one section (or the whole page), choose Blanks, then click each Answer button. Check him against the answer key at the bottom.

A. Underscores (science)

  1. The process plants use to turn sunlight into food is called ________.
  2. Water turning from a liquid into a gas is called ________.
  3. The powerhouse of the cell is the ________.
  4. Earth's closest star is the ________.

B. Answer boxes (history)

  1. The Declaration of Independence was signed in the year .
  2. The first President of the United States was .
  3. The ancient wonder in Egypt built as a tomb for pharaohs is the Great of Giza.

C. Word bank (vocabulary)

Word bank: reluctant · abundant · fragile · ancient

  1. Handle the glass vase carefully; it is very [blank].
  2. After the rain, wildflowers were [blank] across the hillside.
  3. He was [blank] to jump into the cold lake.

D. Two blanks in one sentence (math)

  1. A triangle has ____ sides, and its angles add up to ____ degrees.
  2. In the fraction 3/4, the 3 is the ________ and the 4 is the ________.

E. Tricky ones (should he say he's unsure?)

  1. My favorite color is ________.
  2. The capital of Australia is ________.
Answer key

A. 1. photosynthesis · 2. evaporation · 3. mitochondria · 4. Sun

B. 1. 1776 · 2. George Washington · 3. Pyramid

C. 1. fragile · 2. abundant · 3. reluctant

D. 1. three, 180 · 2. numerator, denominator

E. 1. No right answer: a good reply says it's a personal question, with low confidence. · 2. Canberra (not Sydney, a common mistake).
